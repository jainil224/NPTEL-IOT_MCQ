QUESTION 1
Which type of data analysis is used for data that is categorical in nature?

a. Qualitative analysis
b. Quantitative analysis
c. Regression analysis
d. Analysis of variance  

Correct Answer: a. Qualitative analysis

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Qualitative analysis is used for categorical information, such as colours, types, names, or categories. Quantitative analysis is used for numerical data, such as height, temperature, or weight.

Why this option is correct:
Option (a), Qualitative analysis, is correct because it deals with categorical data.

QUESTION 3
Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) is best applied when which of the following are to be compared?

a. Only a single observation
b. More than two populations or samples
c. Only non-numerical labels without any response variable
d. Only identical copies of the same observation  

Correct Answer: b. More than two populations or samples

Easy Theory / Explanation:
ANOVA stands for Analysis of Variance. It is a statistical technique used to compare datasets, especially when comparing more than two populations or samples. It helps determine whether there are meaningful differences between their group means.

Why this option is correct:
Option (b) is correct because ANOVA is useful for comparing more than two populations or samples.
QUESTION 4
What does Pearson's correlation coefficient (r) measure?

a. The number of equations in a statistical model
b. The maximum value in a dataset
c. The strength of association between two variables
d. The sample size of an experiment  

Correct Answer: c. The strength of association between two variables

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Pearson's correlation coefficient, represented by r, measures the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two quantitative variables. Its value ranges from -1 to +1:
- +1: perfect positive linear relationship.
- 0: no linear relationship.
- -1: perfect negative linear relationship.
Why this option is correct:
Option (c) is correct because Pearson's r measures the association between two variables.
