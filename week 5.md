QUESTION 1
What does interoperability allow IoT devices to do?

a. Communicate and exchange data
b. Work only offline
c. Use only one protocol
d. Disable other devices  

Correct Answer: a. Communicate and exchange data

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Interoperability means that different devices or systems can work together, even when they are made by different manufacturers or use different technologies. In IoT, it lets devices communicate and exchange data.

Why this option is correct:
Option (a) is correct because interoperability helps IoT devices and systems communicate with one another.

Option (a), Message format, describes syntactic interoperability.

QUESTION 12
In the DHT sensor sketch, delay(2000) causes successive readings to be printed after approximately how much time?

a. 0.2 seconds
b. 1 second
c. 2 seconds
d. 20 seconds  

Correct Answer: c. 2 seconds

Easy Theory / Explanation:
In Arduino, the delay() function uses milliseconds. Since 1,000 milliseconds = 1 second, delay(2000) pauses the program for 2,000 milliseconds, which equals 2 seconds.

Why this option is correct:
Option (c), 2 seconds, is correct because 2,000 milliseconds equals 2 seconds.

QUESTION 15
State whether the following statement is True or False.

Statement: A stepper motor is a type of actuator.

a. True
b. False  

Correct Answer: a. True

Easy Theory / Explanation:
An actuator converts energy into physical movement. A stepper motor is an actuator that rotates in small, controlled steps. Other actuators mentioned in the assignment include servo motors, hydraulic motors, solenoids, relays, and AC motors.

Why this option is correct:
Option (a), True, is correct because a stepper motor produces controlled movement.
