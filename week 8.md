QUESTION 1
Which of the following is listed as a problem in traditional (non-SDN) mobile networks?

a. Too flexible hardware architecture
b. Excess centralized control
c. Very low operational cost
d. Difficult to scale due to static, over-provisioned networks  

Correct Answer: d. Difficult to scale due to static, over-provisioned networks

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Traditional mobile networks can be difficult to expand and manage because their network resources are often configured in a fixed way and may be over-provisioned. This makes it harder and more expensive to respond flexibly when mobile traffic demand changes.

Why this option is correct:
Option (d) matches the problem identified in the assignment: traditional networks can be inflexible, difficult to manage, costly, and difficult to scale.
QUESTION 2

a. It is placed at the controller end to manage policies
b. It replaces the OpenFlow protocol entirely

QUESTION 3
What is the primary goal of Mobi-Flow's proactive rule placement approach in Software-Defined IoT?

a. To place flow-rules randomly across all access points
b. To predict users' future location and place flow-rules at APs accordingly
c. To eliminate the need for a controller
d. To increase the number of PACKET-IN messages  

Correct Answer: b. To predict users' future location and place flow-rules at APs accordingly

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Mobi-Flow predicts where a user is likely to be next and places the required flow rules at access points (APs) that may serve that user. The lecture describes predicting the user's location at time \(t+1\), based on the user's location at time \(t\), using an order-K Markov predictor.

Why this option is correct:
Option (b) is correct because proactive rule placement uses predicted future locations to prepare network rules in advance.
