QUESTION 1
In the analogy comparing humans to smart cities presented in the lecture, what do 'Sensors and Tags' correspond to?

a. Skeleton
b. Nerves
c. Sensory Organs
d. Brain  

Correct Answer: c. Sensory Organs

Easy Theory / Explanation:
The lecture compares parts of a human body with parts of a smart city:
- Sensors and tags correspond to sensory organs because they collect information.
- Buildings, industries, and people correspond to the skeleton.
- Digital telecommunication networks correspond to nerves because they carry information.
- Ubiquitously embedded intelligence corresponds to the brain because it helps the city process information and make decisions.
Why this option is correct:
Option (c), Sensory Organs, is correct because sensors and tags collect information from the environment, just as human sensory organs do.

Option (d), Signal level, is the level described for fusion at the sensor node or local network.

QUESTION 15
In the VANET domain classification, which domain consists of vehicles (mobile) and Road-Side Units (RSUs, static), communicating via V2V or V2I using the DSRC stack?

a. In-Vehicle Domain
b. Cloud Domain
c. Infrastructure Domain
d. Ad-hoc Domain  

Correct Answer: d. Ad-hoc Domain

Easy Theory / Explanation:
A VANET is a Vehicular Ad-hoc Network. In the assignment's classification, the Ad-hoc Domain contains mobile vehicles (with On-Board Units, or OBUs) and fixed Road-Side Units (RSUs). They communicate through Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) or Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) links using the DSRC stack (IEEE 802.11p).

Why this option is correct:
Option (d), Ad-hoc Domain, matches the domain described in the source.
