QUESTION 1
According to the lecture, which feature distinguishes a smart grid from a traditional electrical grid?

a. Energy distribution is only one directional.
b. Monitoring and restoration are always performed manually.
c. It enables bidirectional flows of energy with two-way communication and control.
d. Energy generation must occur only at centralized power plants.

Correct Answer: c. It enables bidirectional flows of energy with two-way communication and control.

Easy Theory / Explanation:
A smart grid is a modern electricity network that uses digital technology for monitoring and control. Unlike a traditional grid, it can support energy flowing in both directions and two-way communication between grid components and users.

Why this option is correct:
Option (c) describes the bidirectional energy flow and two-way communication that distinguish a smart grid.

QUESTION 3
Under Time-of-Use pricing, when are consumers encouraged to operate smart appliances to reduce their energy cost?

a. During off-peak hours.
b. Only during on-peak hours.
c. Only when the grid has a blackout.
d. At a fixed time independent of the grid load.

Correct Answer: a. During off-peak hours.

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Time-of-Use pricing means the price of electricity changes depending on the time. Off-peak hours usually have lower demand and lower electricity prices. Consumers can reduce costs by running suitable appliances during these hours.

Why this option is correct:
Option (a), During off-peak hours, is correct because the assignment says electricity is generally cheaper when grid load is lower.
QUESTION 4
In net metering, the consumer's final bill depends on which two energy flows?

a. Reactive power and communication traffic.
b. Only the energy generated at centralized power plants.
c. Only the energy stored in the consumer's battery.
d. Energy inflow from the grid and energy outflow from the consumer to the grid.

Correct Answer: d. Energy inflow from the grid and energy outflow from the consumer to the grid.

Easy Theory / Explanation:
Net metering accounts for the electricity a consumer takes from the grid and excess electricity the consumer sends back to the grid, for example from a rooftop solar system. These two energy flows help determine the bill.

Why this option is correct:
Option (d) includes both the energy received from the grid and the energy supplied back to it.

QUESTION 15
In Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS), which node stores the actual data blocks?

a. Datanode.
b. Namenode.
c. Job Tracker.
d. YARN Resource Negotiator only.

Correct Answer: a. Datanode.

Easy Theory / Explanation:
HDFS stores large files by dividing them into blocks and distributing those blocks across Datanodes. The Namenode maintains filesystem metadata, such as information about files and where their blocks are stored.

Why this option is correct:
Option (a), Datanode, stores the actual data blocks, while the Namenode manages metadata.
